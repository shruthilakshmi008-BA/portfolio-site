How I work
About

Working at the intersection of business, process, data, and technology.

Shruthi's role starts with understanding a business problem, not a system. She runs stakeholder discovery, maps how work actually happens today, and translates that into requirements a technical team can build against with minimal rework — then stays through UAT and production support to make sure what shipped actually solves the problem.

Her background in software engineering and automation isn't the headline — it's what lets her sit in a room with engineers and finance stakeholders in the same afternoon and be precise with both. That combination shows up across healthcare, retail, and supply-chain engagements, from HIPAA-governed practice-management platforms to SQL-driven revenue reconciliation.
